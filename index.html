About

Hi, I'm Swati.

I've spent the last few years designing and building websites for small businesses that need more than a generic template — brand sites, online stores, and everything in between. I work across Figma, WordPress, Shopify and the Adobe suite, depending on what the project actually needs, not what's trendiest.

I like projects where the client has a real story to tell. My job is figuring out how to make people stop scrolling long enough to hear it.
